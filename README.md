# Hear Clock

Tap the clock on your Android home screen to hear the local time. Hear Clock is a resizable widget for people who are blind or have low vision.

[Download for Android 8.0 or later](https://github.com/byfb/hear-clock/releases/latest) · [繁體中文](README.zh-Hant.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-Hans.md)

Open Hear Clock, tap **Add widget**, and confirm. With TalkBack, focus the clock on your home screen and double-tap to hear the time.
