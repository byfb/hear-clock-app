## v0.4.0 — 2026-11-19

Hear Clock’s menus and TalkBack labels are now available in English, Simplified Chinese (简体中文), and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文).

### Added

- Choose a language under **Settings → Appearance → App language**. By default, Hear Clock follows your phone’s language. On Android 13 and later, you can also change it in Android’s app language settings.
- Clock face options, color names, and speech controls are translated. TalkBack also reads the clock’s label and settings in the selected language.

### Upgrade notes

- Install `hear-clock-0.4.0.apk` over v0.3.2 to keep your home-screen widget, clock appearance, and voice settings.
- To hear the time in a different language from the menus, choose **Settings → Voice → Speech language**. The **Device language** voice follows your phone. Your phone’s 12- or 24-hour preference still applies when the clock format is set to **Device**.
