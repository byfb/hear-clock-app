## v0.3.2 — 2026-11-19

This patch fixes a blank home-screen clock reproduced on Android 15.

### Fixed

- Digital and stacked clocks now fit the space the launcher gives them. The digits stay visible when the launcher reports a larger size or resizes an existing widget.
- The full clock face responds to taps, including its edges. Hear Clock refreshes the face and its speech action after you add a widget, update the app, or open Hear Clock.

### Upgrade notes

- Install `hear-clock-0.3.2.apk` over v0.3.1 to keep your clock settings and the widget already on your home screen.
- If your widget was blank, open Hear Clock once after installing the update, then return to the home screen and tap the clock to hear the time.
