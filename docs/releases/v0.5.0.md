## v0.5.0 — 2026-11-20

You can now choose what you hear when you tap the clock. Open **Settings → Voice** to change it.

### Voice settings

- Turn off **Speak on tap** for a silent clock. Android TalkBack can still read the time when you focus the clock.
- Turn on **Include today’s date** to hear the time followed by the weekday and full date in your chosen voice language. Tap **Test voice** to hear it at your selected speed.

Tap **Save settings** to apply your choices to the app and the widgets already on your home screen.

### Upgrade notes

Install `hear-clock-0.5.0.apk` over v0.4.1. The APK uses the same signing key, so your widgets and settings carry over. The clock starts with tap-to-speak on and the date off, just as it sounded before the update.
