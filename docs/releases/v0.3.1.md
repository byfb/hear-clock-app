## v0.3.1 — 2026-11-19

This is the first GitHub release of Hear Clock for Android. The source for this APK is available under GPL-3.0-only. [Download the corresponding source code](https://github.com/byfb/hear-clock/archive/refs/tags/v0.3.1.zip).

### Added

- Tap anywhere on the resizable home-screen clock to hear the local time, or focus it with TalkBack and double-tap.
- Choose a digital, stacked, or analog face and adjust its colors, digits, or individual clock hands to suit your vision.
- Choose an installed speech language, adjust the playback speed, and preview the voice before saving it to the app and widget.

### Upgrade notes

- Download `hear-clock-0.3.1.apk` under **Assets** and open it on a phone running Android 8.0 or later.
- If you installed an earlier test APK, note your clock settings, uninstall that copy, and install this release. Android requires a fresh install for the new signing key. Uninstalling clears the old settings and removes its home-screen widget.
